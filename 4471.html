5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

  • Shared parser. The parser reads numeric input month first. For a tenant whose locale puts the day first, a date such as 07/04/2026 can land on 4 July when the tenant meant 7 April. Report R-41200 describes that reversal. Parse according to the tenant's date order.
  • Booking-v3 form hint. The form shows MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants, while the form interprets dates day first for those tenants. A customer following the hint can type 01/06/2026 for 6 January and get 1 June. R-41230 describes that outcome. Make the hint match the date order the form accepts.

The one-day moves in the table stay in their current group for this count. The desk flagged them for follow-up because the reports do not establish a common cause for those symptoms.

When the reports reached the desk

Reports started arriving in April. The first merges came in July. Keep the date of the first report visible alongside the merge date so that the merge backlog does not hide how long customers had been affected.
